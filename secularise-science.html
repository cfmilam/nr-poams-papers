Secularise science

N. Vivian Pope

Should Modern Physics and Cosmology be Re-Secularised Physics, originally known as Natural Philosophy was an entirely secular undertaking, designed to unearth natural truth – that is to say, to discover the correct, most logically efficient and coherent interpretations of natural phenomena.

In its best days, researchers such as Copernicus, Galileo, Kepler et al. simply observed how things worked, how they influenced one another and so on. For this they used logic and mathematics to ensure the objectivity of their findings. They experimented with bits of matter in all sorts of different positions and situations, mixing various chemical substances and meticulously recording the results.

In all of this they may well have assumed that they were studying ‘God’s handiwork’ – indeed, how else could they suppose that what they were studying was not some mere figment of their own subjective imagination. In this way, physical science was characterised as purely objective, as dealing with the world in the world’s own terms, in an open-ended way, with physical reality being something that reveals itself gradually and empirically, with all human precepts, or theories regarding it kept at the barest minimum.

Completely opposite to this was the notion that God’s intentions for the world were already known and revealed in the Bible and in prayer, and that the aim of scientific endeavour should be to cram all empirical knowledge and thought into conformity with those religious precepts.

The guiding principle, therefore, was to ensure that all scientific probings into nature must not lead us astray from what the priests regarded as revealed truth. Little did these priests realise that this ‘revealed truth’ of theirs existed nowhere but inside their own heads.

With the backing of the Supreme Authority of the Church, these purveyors of religious knowledge saw it as their aim to instil in the minds of an ignorant public an Authoritarian regime which brooked no criticism. A typical example of this pressure towards conformity was the case of Galileo’s attempt, in 1609, by means of the newly developed telescope, to demonstrate to his peers the phenomenon of spots on the sun.

This demonstration immediately ran into trouble with the Catholic Church, whose authorities regarded it as blasphemy to suggest that God had created a ‘flawed luminary’. As they saw it, if that was what the new-fangled ‘telescope’ showed, then it had to be an ‘instrument of the devil’.

For this and other such transgressions, Galileo had to be punished. Others following in this same completely secular line of enquiry were ‘put to the Question’ by the Catholic Inquisition, some of them to be threatened with torture, like Galileo, and others actually tortured, like Giordano Bruno who, together with all his ‘heretical’ writings. was burned at the stake.

In short, anything contradicting or even seeming to contradict what, in their heads, these authorities presumed to be the revealed ‘Word of God’ had to be smothered out of existence. For the length of time that this authoritarian regime lasted, scientific progress was virtually halted on its tracks.

Religious doctrine ‘ruled okay’, and only on pain of death could anyone seek to subvert it. Following the inevitable demise of that regime, true Physics emerged from its funk hole and, for a while, was able to pursue its original aim of observing, logically interpreting and recording natural phenomena,

unencumbered by religious precepts. This so-called ‘Age of Reason’, however, was relatively short lived, succeeded as it is, nowadays, by a resurgence of that same stultifying Religious Authoritarianism which, in the name of ‘Modern Physics’. is no more than a re-make of the religious dogmas of the past.

No less constraining is that it practically forbids any ideas which do not conform to the pattern set by the dogma of the ‘Big Bang’ origin of Creation and its plethora of supporting theories about ‘gravity waves’, ‘gravitons’, particles of ‘dark matter’, ‘black holes’, the ‘God Particle’ and so on, However, for any free-range philosophical thinker it is plain that there is a flaw in the Big Bang theory of Cosmic Creation, right at its very foundation.

This fault lies in the assumption that the universe of galaxies is seen to be expanding, hence that, by present calculations, some fourteen billion years ago it must all have exploded into being, into an unspeakable nothing, from a point called the ‘primeval atom’.

Some thinkers feel that in a democracy they should be free to regard this as just about the silliest idea that was ever conceived. The true fact, they will say, is that the universe is by no means seen to be expanding. that what is actually seen is an overall reddening of the light in the spectra of the distant galaxies.

From this so-called Cosmological Redshift. the speed of that galactic recession is inferred, similarly to the way in which a ‘Doppler’ police camera measures the speed of a travelling road vehicle. The classic logical fallacy this commits is to assume that because all receding light-sources exhibit a redshift, seeing a light-source redshifted is the same as seeing that light-source receding – which is like saying that because all dogs are quadrupeds, seeing a quadruped is the same as seeing a dog.

Nevertheless, despite this rather obvious flaw, right at the root of the reasoning upon which practically the whole edifice of Modern Physics and Cosmology depends, it is automatically inferred that the galaxies are receding, at speeds proportional to their distance, hence that the whole cosmos is expanding.

Within this presumptive Belief-System, the grandeur of our modern temples of Physics, such as CERN, Fermilab, Brookhaven etc., easily matches the splendour of the bejewelled Medieval churches, while their in-group vernacular of mathematics is every bit as exclusive of common understanding as the Latin used by the priests of yore.

For these modern priests of the Big Bang Dogma, mathematics, far from being regarded as a tool of open-ended secular discovery, defines an end-state towards which all discovery must be directed, a Super-Realm of Reality existing independently of ordinary reality in some projected Platonic Empyrean identified with – well, what else but ‘the Supreme Mathematician, God’.

Accordingly, all talk, on the upper echelons of contemporary Authoritarian Physics, is not about an open-minded exploration of nature but what our Leucasian professor, Steven Hawking and others describe as ‘reading the mind of God’. Meanwhile, the designers of the LHC (Large Hadron Collider) at CERN describe their corporate aim in constructing this hugely expensive apparatus as searching for the ‘God Particle’, technically called the ‘Higgs boson’, that they believe to have accompanied the first explosive microseconds of ‘God’s Creation’.

So, as opposed to freely interpreting natural phenomena, whereas the medieval priests saw their first duty as being to interpret the Holy Scriptures, their modern

counterparts, the Big Bang Theorists, see their aim as being to interpret the Holy Equations. The constraints on the natural progress of Physical Science imposed by our Modern Mathematical Physics are therefore every bit as stultifying as those of its medieval predecessor.

Aided by the uncritical, self-serving public media, this Dogma dominates the conceptual terrain like a roaring theoretical ‘Tyrannosaurus Rex’. Meanwhile, good physics continues in the background – underground, one might say, like the mammals who survived the extinction of the dinosaurs.

All it takes to quell the current wave of theoretical insanity and bring natural physics back into the sunlight of normality is for Commonsense to have had its ‘sickener’ of the atrocious nonsense peddled by the Priests of Modern Theoretical Physics and Cosmology and awake, like the sleeping giant to realise the vanity and extravagance – and sheer pointlessness – of such rampant and expensive nonsense as seeking for ‘God Particles’ and so on.

For natural philosophy to regain its status in the public eye as something intrinsically interesting above the usual earth-bound mediocrities of Politics, Advertising, Sport, Celebrity-worship and the like, it has to break, once more, the shackles of Authoritarian Religion and follow freely on its original empirical course, with obedience to no rules but those of plain commonsense logic and language.

As soon as our Physics and Cosmology get back on that rational track we will surely see, in the hindsight of history, this present era of God-seeking science as more like some devilish joke. In view of the spectacular non-events following the switch-on of the monstrous LHC at CERN, which had threatened to re-create the ‘Big Bang’ and its accompanying ’Black Hole’, this restoration of reason in matters of Physics may come sooner rather than later.

Neville Vivian (Viv) Pope. Science-Philosopher and Research Associate of the Maths Department, Keele University, UK. --------------------------------------------------------------------------NASA has discovered inexplicable anomalies in the or orbital trajectories of their space-probes.
