To all in the NPA: My Final Analysis of ‘Dissidence’ Viv Pope I feel that the exercise which started-off all this 'dissidence' stuff has (for me, at any rate) now run its course. However, it would not be fitting for me to have started the thing off, as I did in 2001, simply to disappear without some concluding remarks. So here, for whatever it is worth, is my objective and unbiased analysis of this burgeoning phenomenon of ‘Dissidence’. For me, the exercise has uncovered two main divisions of thinkers. One consists of those who can accept without demur, an approach to knowledge in general (and scientific knowledge in particular) which confines itself to just one spatial location at a time, i.e., the viewpoint of the finite ‘observer'. In physical science this is not necessarily a human being but may be anything in one’s observational frame of reference, from one’s scientific colleagues to instruments of some sort, such as a telescope, microscope, camera, detector or some other physical entity in the armoury of the scientific researcher, whose job it is to interpret all this Informational input in the most logically efficient way. In this radically empirical, or observational approach, the only knowledge we may ever have of other things and other people, their physiological constitutions, masses and other dimensions, their thoughts, chemical compositions, ageing-rates and so on, has to be from what we personally observe of their locations, dispositions, behaviour and so on with regard to ourselves – in short, from our physical interactions and communications with people and things as they relate to one another and, ultimately, to ourselves. Let this be called the Principle of the Finitude and Relativity of Knowledge or, as it is traditionally called, Empiricism. The other division of protagonists consists of those who cannot accept this principle of spatio-temporal confinement and who feel that the only truly scientific way of describing the world is from a completely depersonalised, 'non-subjectivist' (so-called 'Realist') standpoint which releases them from this spatio-temporal confinement into a Realm of what

they regard as ‘Pure Reason’. From the standpoint of this approach, natural laws and their accompanying mathematical formulae have absolute and impersonal significance. This is the approach to Natural Philosophy that is classically known as Platonic Rationalism, or Absolutism which their Empiricist opponents describe as ‘God's-eyeview realism' or 'Metaphysical Realism'. Logically, these two fundamentally divided Empiricist and Absolutist approaches are, as ever, logically irreconcilable. One might therefore as usefully discuss issues of outright religion as discuss issues of 'science' involving these two implacably opposed approaches to the subject. This is why, in these 'Dissident' discussions, words like 'rational' and 'irrational', 'logical' and 'illogical', 'commonsensical', 'nonsensical' ... and so on are bandied about with no more consensus as to their meaning than might be found in an aviary of language-trained parrots and mynah birds. The fact of the matter, as I see it, is that the Establishment has espoused relativism of the Empiricist sort described above, whereas the 'Dissidents' (in the NPA, at any rate) are the Absolutists who have polarised themselves against those Establishment Empiricists or Relativists. This, as I now clearly see, is why my attempts to reconcile the two viewpoints, which originally set off all this controversy (after a talk I gave in the University of Connecticut, Storrs) were doomed to fail and which, as I am now convinced, defeats any point in pursuing it further. In this present 'Dissidence' context it would be like trying to reconcile Conservative and Liberal (Republican and Democrat in the USA). However, from what has transpired so far, it seems that there is little recognition of this wider, ‘bird’s-eye-view’ state of things. It is particular ironic, therefore, to discover that the phrase ‘Natural Philosophy’ as applied to the so-called ‘Natural Philosophy Association’, is a complete misnomer. Among the discussions conducted under this title, scarcely any awareness of what ‘Natural Philosophy’’ truly means can be discerned. Nor is there any more trace of understanding of what ‘logic’ means, far less how a logical argument should be conducted. These, at any rate, are my unbiased findings in the matter, the result one’s honest

So fanatical is the continued diatribe against ‘Einstein’ and ‘SR’ that those who engage in it can scarcely contemplate any interpretation of relativity other than Einstein’s and the whole mess of ancillary ideas associated with the names of people like Lorentz, Maxwell, and others whose names are connected with the nineteenth century kerfuffle over what light is, how it travels and how it is conducted, by ‘fields’, ‘ethers’ and so on. There seems to be, for some people, no hope of rescue from that nineteenth century rut in order to contemplate any other, more radical and up-to-date interpretation of the relevant facts. These people seem to be afflicted with a condition of conceptual entrenchment, a hardening of the mental arteries, as it were, against any flow of ideas other than those aimed at ’disproving Einstein’, as though that were the sole point and purpose of what they regard as ‘science’. For them there is only that one kind of ‘dissidence’, ignoring the fact that dissidence, in a whole variety of forms, has always been at the ‘cutting edge’ of true scientific progress. To learn that ‘Einstein’ has been overtaken by Bondi and others is something they don’t wish to know, as it would only ‘spoil the game’ – the game of hide and seek with ideas of ‘Einstein’, ‘light-velocity’ and ‘Relativity’. This militates against any attempt to broaden their philosophical horizons, and this is truly abysmal when there are much bigger games afoot at the cutting-edge of current empirical physics and cosmology. More than anything, there seems to be a complete ignorance of the fact that what is now called ’Physics’ is actually a peculiarly ingrained form of philosophy. This makes a complete irony of the presence of that word in the title of the ‘NPA’. That particular philosophy presently called ‘Physics’ is just one among others which might have – and indeed still might – replace it, such as Mach’s non-Einsteinian relativistic philosophy, for example. Undoubtedly, this would require a whole new register of language, with our now familiar words like ‘electron’, ‘field-force’, ‘lightvelocity’, ‘electrodynamics’ and the whole range of associated languageusages (pace Wittgenstein) becoming defunct. Such is the course of evolution, of science and language as well as of biological species. To suggest on that philosophical scale of things, something along the lines of what John Anderson of NASA projects as the need for a ‘New Physics’

would be true dissidence, Unfortunately, it would have to be of a kind which present, card-carrying ‘Dissidents’ can scarcely contemplate, miredin as they mostly are with their nineteenth century thought-processes, esp;ecially when the ‘New Physics’ Andrews talks about has little to do with Einsteinian Relativity, either Special or General. As things stand, then, in view of the record of most of my contacts with members of this group, I cannot imagine what sense they will make of this present missive, even if they can read it. I live in hope of someone in the Association being capable of convincing me that those who have instilled in me my lack of confidence in – indeed in despair of – their rationality are not representative of the group as a whole. To anyone who can do that I will apologise unconditionally for the negativity of this report. Otherwise, it stands firmly as it is. Sorry, folks, but my only motive here, believe it or not, is to ’speak as I find’. This is in the forlorn hope that it may help to elevate these discussions out of their narrow anti-Einstein groove to seek scientific truth on a less biased and more objective philosophical level as befits the title of the Natural Philosophy Association. I am a born Natural Philosopher and this is what Natural Philosophers do. Best personal wishes to all, Viv Pope.

P.S. I should say that this exercise has been, for me, not entirely negative. It has been like mining for pitchblende, producing a very small yet hugely valuable yield from a gigantic pile of garbage. To that extent, my efforts in kick-starting this thread of discussion in circa 2000, have been personally fruitful, especially in view of some feedback I have received on my Angular Momentum Synthesis from personal contacts and conferences I have attended. For the most part, however, all my contributions to the discussion have, at best, been met with the most paltry ad hominem attempts simply to rubbish them. Some of the older members may recall that this was my reason for dissociating from the group during the Chappell era of administration. Unfortunately, so far as I am concerned, the promise of a new wholesale shake-up and broadening
