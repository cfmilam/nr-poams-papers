In this instance, the direction of the satellite’s spin is counter to that of the earth’s rotation. In this case, the satellite is spinning in the direction opposite to that of the earth’s rotation (retrograde spin). Note that the effect of this negative spin, in magnitude terms, is the same as in positive terms. That is to say, in these magnitude terms, the vectors have no effect whatsoever.

CONCLUSION: The same goes for any other vector orientation or composition of vectors. The amounts are the amounts, regardless of vector orientations. This follows from the conservation of magnitudes.

The question, then, remains: What effect on the orbits, if any, do the vectors have?
