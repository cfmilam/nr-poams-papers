So my difficulty, Curt, which I fourd to be insuperable without creating logical mayhem, was how to continue with that purely Newtonian dsrivation without having to accept time-dilation as some sort of physical reality.

This is why, having held out for years against time-dilation, I was eventually forced, by sheer logic, and against all my prior'intuitions, to accept it. However, that did not mean having to accept the orthodox, Einstein-Lorentz version of timedilation, as people like Ron Hatch and George Galeczki assume.

My alternative derivation is essentially neo-Newtonian (or neo-Galilean, if you prefer). The crucial difference is that in my derivation there is no 'light-limit c', hence no 'Einstein-separation'between bodies at a distance and therefore no nonsense about 'EPR' and so on.

All real-time (proper-time) motions are Galilean, and it is only the relative motions that are time-dilated. And it is on the level of those real-time motions, not the relative-time motions, that my angular momenhrm 'instantaneous action-at-a-distance'thesis comes into play, as what people like Selleri call the'reality underlying the relativistic appearances'.

Of course, having to accept relativistic tirne-dilation as a physical fact meant my having to revise the language according to which I had previously labelled these relativistic effects as 'apparent' as opposed to the others which I had labelled'real'.

That distinction was, plainly, no longer the mot juste. So, eventually, I abandoned this rather inept dichotomy and settled for a philosophical position called Normal Realisrn, according to which the proper-times and the relative times of physical objects are both real but in different, yet complementary ways, ways, like the reality of myself as I personally experience it and the reality of that same self as you see me from your own remove as a separate being.
