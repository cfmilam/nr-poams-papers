Modern Physics, plan B

N. Vivian Pope

A Fifty-Year Fallback for a Failed Modern Physics by Prof. N. V. (Viv) Pope It is now fairly certain that the Large Hadron Collider (LHC) experiment at CERN to confirm the existence of the Higgs boson as evidence of the occurrence of the theoretical Big Bang, has failed. Funding for these hugely expensive ‘atom smashing’ projects is drying up as more and more sponsors lose confidence in their very aims and intentions. The more forcefully these theorists attempt to justify their ‘Big Science’ via the public media, the wider becomes the commonsense recognition of its flaws. For instance, as perceptive readers of the Internet will know, it has been logically established that the notion of the ‘expanding universe’ on which the Big Bang theory is founded is a fallacy – namely the idea that because all receding lightsources are redshifted, all light-sources showing a redshift, such as the distant galaxies are receding, a classic example of logical error. This is why Big Science, as conducted in atom-smashing laboratories like the Tevatron at Fermilab is now gradually being phased out, starved as it is of funding from sponsors as belief in its purpose becomes more and more eroded. This, together with the appearance of insoluble anomalies like the Pioneer Anomaly and the Missing Mass Anomaly, despite all the contrived attempts to cover for them with bizarre theories of ‘dark matter’, dark energy’ and so on, makes it plain that the Standard Model of physics and cosmology with which we have become familiar over the last decades is now falling apart at the seams. Most of us in the science community who imbibed that ‘Plan A’ paradigm practically with our mother’s milk are now left mentally adrift, bereft of any fallback position to contemplate, hence of anything truly sensible to think or say about the situation. This would be an unmitigated disaster, were it not for the fact that behind the scenes there has been developing, steadily, over the past half century, what might fairly be called a fallback Plan B’ approach to our failing Plan-A. This Plan-B was introduced originally, by the philosopher-physicist Ernst Mach, in the tradition of English Empiricism that was forged throughout the seventeenth century by Locke, Berkeley and Hume. Mach vigorously rejected the concept of ‘atom-smashing’ and all its conceptual infrastructure as what he regarded as an entirely mistaken approach to physics. For him, that reductionist, or atomist approach to natural philosophy was as vain in its intentions as smashing-up chessmen to reveal the rules of chess. The true basis of physics, for Mach, was not the behind-the-scenes theoretical sub-world of ‘atoms and void’ that had been imagined by Democritus and become institutionalised in physics by the likes of Descartes, Galileo and Newton. It was obvious to Mach, as to anyone who thinks about it, that more fundamental than that – most fundamental of all, in fact – in the attainment of knowledge of nature is the actual observational, upfront data of scientific sensation and instrumentation. It was this so-called ‘phenomenalism’, stressing the absolute priority of informational data over assumptions about atoms and so on, which we may regard as the philosophical Plan-B of physics. It was this ‘phenomenalism’ of Mach’s that supplied the inspiration for his protégé, Einstein’s, famous Special Theory of Relativity – albeit imperfectly, so far as Mach was concerned. In this fallback Physics Plan-B there, is no distance, no time nor any other physical dimension in and of the void or vacuum as such. For Mach and his latest

followers, who have been dubbed the neo-Machians, or Normal Realists, physical phenomena with their defining dimensions, properties, qualities, etc., are essentially relative. That is to say, they are projected out of directly perceived informational patterns and sequences of quantum data in a manner similar to the way in which, from patterns and sequences of lighted screen pixels, a viewer projects the breadth, depth and action of a video scenario. In this way Mach’s ‘Plan-B’ of physics incorporates, in one and the same philosophical approach, the basic principles of both Relativity and Quantum Theory, without any hint of the notorious conflict between the two that was waged between Albert Einstein and Niels Bohr, well known in to physicists as the chronic EPR (Einstein, Podolski, Rosen) paradox. The most illustrious exponents of the traditional Plan A paradigm were, of course, Galileo, Newton, Kepler, et al. who, between them, defined the field of study for our pre-twenty-first century physics. This twentieth-century theoretical field of study was conceived as a self-sufficient continuum, frame or vacuum, of three absolute length dimensions, all enduring in a universal ‘God’s time’ which advances in parallel, the same everywhere all at once, the same in the vacuum of space as in matter. By contrast, the field of study according to the Neo-Machian Plan-B is a discontinuum of quantum pixels transacted directly between objects, including, of course, human observers. Each of these pixels has the dimensions of Planck’s quantum h, and they occur in statistical numbers in interactive, holographic or pictographic arrays like the patterns and sequences of ‘blips’ on a TV screen, in the manner already described. This, according to our neo-Machian Plan-B, is the physical world relative to the observer. It would be identical to the space-time substratum of Einstein’s Special Theory of Relativity if it were not for the fact that in this Plan-B relativity, Einstein’s Second Axiom postulating the ‘constant speed of light in vacuo’ is omitted. Indeed, in this contender for the position of the anticipated New Physics, the whole notion of light as something ‘travelling in space’ is rejected as a relic of Plan-A which is not only surplus to requirements but is also fundamentally false. The fallacy lies in what is commonly interpreted as ‘the ‘speed of light’ in space. Speed is defined as a measure of the motion of a material body, namely, the distance travelled by that body with respect to some chosen point of reference in a measured time. There is no speed, properly so-called, which can be constant relative to a vacuum - i.e., a nothing, or void. Nor can there be a speed which is the same with regard to different points if reference which are in motion. The ‘speed of light’ in Einstein’s theory, however, is the same with regard to different – indeed all – points of reference. Ergo, it cannot logically be a speed. A measure which is the same in all reference-frames, is a constant, not a speed. Properly speaking, a constant is a constant like 2.2 pounds to the kilogram, or 36.37 inches to the metre, which is the same for all observers in all states of motion or rest; and a speed is a speed, which is not the same for all observers in relative motion or rest. To conflate the two is a confusion, and this is one of the prime fallacies which lie right at the heart of Plan-A Physics. It makes Einsteinian Relativity a theoretical hybrid between two logically incommensurable thoughtsystems, which explains why ‘Relativity’, since its introduction by Einstein in 1905, has remained a music-hall buzzword for intellectual obscurity. Moreover, this very same theory which asserts that light travels in vacuo (Einstein’s Second Axiom of Special Relativity) also asserts that light is nothing – literally, no thing. To be any thing (a ‘photon’, say) that thing must have mass, else it is nothing. But according to the theory, at the ‘speed of light’ the mass of anything becomes infinite, which is a paradox. Physicists hedge this paradox by arguing that in

its own rest-frame, the ‘photon’ must have zero mass. But it is axiomatic in Einstein’s Theory that the ‘photon’ is never at rest but has the ‘speed c’ in all reference-frames bar none. In no way, therefore, can light be anything (i.e., a particle’) travelling in vacuo. And as for ‘waves’ in vacuo what can possibly wave in a vacuum? The assumption that light has a velocity c in vacuo, for all that it is intuitively appealing, is logically untenable. As a basis for physics, this fallacy in itself would be bad enough. But there is more. Another fallacious Plan-A postulate is that all bodies without any net external force acting upon them travel in straight lines (Newton’s so-called First Law of Motion). In fact, however, nowhere, are free-moving bodies observed to travel in straight lines. In all cases they travel in curves. Rectilinear free, ‘inertial’ motion is pure fiction, or, to say the least, unempirical. By contrast, the eminent fourth century BC Greek scientist and logician, Aristotle, observed what we all observe and told it just as it is, namely, that all force-free motion is curved. This was confirmed by his reasoning, which told him that continuous free motion in a straight line is impossible. A continuous straight-line motion has to begin and end somewhere, in which case it is interrupted, i.e., non-continuous; otherwise it has to be infinitely long, which is impossible. The only continuous free motion therefore, Aristotle concluded, has to be cyclic or orbital – which, of course, is puts logical reason and observation together in the way that was to become the model of reasoning for all future science. The trouble with Newton’s unempirical ‘straight-line’ conception of continuous force-free motion is that, logically, it implies that any body which is seen to be travelling in a curve, such as every planet, satellite, comet etc., in our solar system, has some kind of invisible force acting upon it. In this way is conjured-up, as if by magic, a whole plethora of in vacuo ‘field-forces’ of one sort or another to explain all the various kinds of curvilinear motions that scientific observation and experiment reveal. Thus is created the magical world of abstract mathematical entities and processes that is our customary Physics Plan A. The first of these fictitious in vacuo ’field-forces’ that falls to mind is, of course Newton’s ‘force of gravity’. According to Newton’s ‘Universal Law of Gravitation’, any two objects in the Universe exert a gravitational attraction on each other with the force which is the product of their masses divided by the square of the distance between them, all multiplied by an empirical factor, G, called the ‘universal gravitational constant’. It is this invisible ‘gravitational force’ which, according to Newton, pulls on the masses of the bodies to make them orbit each other against the ‘centrifugal force’ which seeks to make them fly off to travel in straight lines. In this way of thinking, the two equal and opposite forces, the ‘gravitational’ and the ‘centrifugal’, annul each other, so that the total force acting on the orbiting body is nil. In this way, it takes Newton two theoretical moves to get where Aristotle got in just one in explaining orbital motion. It is to be noted, here, that Newton’s formalism takes no account of the spin of an orbiting body. By default, the ‘gravitational force’ between the two bodies is assumed to be the same, regardless of whether or not the bodies are spinning. This falls foul of the law of angular momentum conservation according to which the total orbital angular momentum of a free-moving body must include that of any spin it might have along with any other form of angular momentum, such as oscillation, etc. The fact that none of these forms of angular momentum is listed in the overall amount of orbital plus spin is a glaring anomaly in view of the enormous amount of spin angular momentum that exists in the universe, from fundamental particles to stars and

planets including, not least, the prodigious amount of spin that is contained in the multitudes of spiral galaxies. Even NASA’s space-probes spin, and since this spin is ignored in the Newtonian ‘gravitational’ equations the NASA engineers use in calculating the trajectories of their probes, the amount of orbital angular momentum, minus the spin will be less than expected, with the result that the probe will orbit ‘anomalously’ nearer the sun than it should according to the equations. This, of course, is precisely what is observed in what is called the ‘Pioneer anomaly’. By the same token, calculating the total mass of the ‘universe’ by those ‘gravitational’ ‘equations without taking account of the immense amount of spin that there is out there will inevitably make it seem that there is some very large anomalous proportion of ‘missing mass’. This creates the anomaly that some astrophysicists have bizarrely explained as manifesting the existence of invisible and undetectable ‘dark matter’ or ‘dark energy’. In fact, the whole history of our traditional Plan-A Physics is shot through with fallacies of this kind, in the contrived explanations of which theoretical extremism is proliferating at a rate of knots. The mindset appropriate to this fallback physics, called Normal Realism, is, as might be expected, very different from that which, nurtured in the context of our traditional Plan-A Physics has been called ‘commonsense’. In particular, there can be, logically, no monolithic ‘Universe’ as such. There can be only a ‘Pluriverse’ of indeterminately interacting entities of one kind or another, with there being no possible simultaneous or ubiquitous overview of the age or state of ‘the Whole’ at any observational instant. That is to say, there is no temporal cross-section of the ‘Whole’ that can sensibly be regarded as the universal ‘Present Moment’. This dispenses with the traditional ’God’s Eye View’ of physical reality, assumed by the likes of Herbert Dingle and his followers, which has militated against Relativity since that theory’s inception. It is by no means difficult to see these absolutists, or self-styled ‘Dissidents’, as being of the same reactionary mindset as the cardinals of the Catholic Church who persecuted those revolutionary thinkers of the time, like Copernicus and Galileo who they saw as threatening the standardised view of our Earth as the hub of the cosmos and the centre of God’s Creation? Needless to say, this view now seems ridiculous in the context of what our modern telescopes and other instruments reveal to us as the size and depth of extraterrestrial space. What is there to guarantee that our present Standard Model of Physics and Cosmology is not destined to be appear as equally ridiculous in hindsight – if, indeed, it has not already done so at present? This fallback Plan-B Physics has been offered for publication in the mainstream science journals but has constantly been refused. This is scarcely surprisingly, perhaps, since it would require a lot of nerve on the part of the publisher to become party to such a heresy. which would be like asking the Vatican to publish articles on the use of condoms. Answers to requests by NASA for explanations of the anomalous trajectories of their space-probes have been monolithically ignored, even though notice of receipt was recorded. The answer we gave was simply that the anomaly lay nowhere but in the neglect of spin in Newton’s ‘gravitational’ equations which NASA was using in calculating their orbital trajectories. No sensible discussion of this proposal has been observed. Reports as to the existence of this solution in the lists of proposed explanations somehow mysteriously disappeared. However, a manuscript has now been completed encapsulating the full fiftyyear development of this ‘Plan-B’ enterprise. The manuscript is entitled ‘Einstein’s Lost Legacy’ by N. V. Pope and A. D. Osborne, and those who have read it report that

it is very impressive, that it presents an argument that is logically consistent, coherent and understandable. Indeed, some have judged that it is ‘irrefutable’. However, for all that, some see it as a ‘dangerous book’, an ‘immoral book’ and a ‘modern heresy’. Such bits of it as have been published piecemeal on the Internet have met with hostile reactions, some of which have been incredible – even from those who regard themselves as ‘Dissidents’ One of my granddaughters, reading what one critic had said about having to wash his hands in bleach after touching the work responded with ‘Oh, Grampa, you must be doing something powerful to create that sort of reaction!’ The manuscript is presently in the hands of a recommended publisher. Let’s see whether they will publish it or ‘wash their hands of it – in bleach’.
