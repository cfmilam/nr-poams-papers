Abstract Instantaneous action-at-adistance and the finite-speed of light are generally considered antithetical. However, it is well known that in relativistic physics light has both a firute and an infinite speed. Logically, then, we may think of the non-local quantum components of light-interaction as occurring in terms of the proper time of that interaction, in which the speed is instantaneous, and of the light signals, or waves, as a local process consisting of cinematic sequences of these 'quantum stills' in the relative or observer time of the action, in which the speed is finite.

This logical synthesis of locality and non-locality requires replacing the propagational field-electrodynamics of Faraday and Maxwell by the direct, non-field tlpe of interaction of Weber and Helmholtz, as preferred by Feynman and Wheeler, Chubykalo and Smirnov-Rueda. It also requires replacing the traditional concepts of gravity and electric force by that of instantaneous balancing-moments between masses and spinning masses, respectively, in overall-conserved angular momentum relations.

This is in some sense afield like that of historical electrodynamics, except that there is no underlying continuum and there are no 'electric charges' as sources and sinks of the propagated energy. In the P-O theory, therefore, there is no need of measures in coulombs. The 'charges' are re-interpreted as energies injoules, which are energies either ofmotion or potential for motion, thus dispensing with any need to think of the particles possessing a kind of 'static' metaphysically 'fizzing away' in the void. The problem ofthe age, then, so far as physics is concerned is that relativity requires that no physical influence can travel faster than c, while quantum physics requires that there are influences that travel at an infinite or instantaneous speed. That should, logically, synthesise the two theories

and dispose, at a stroke, of the apparent 'spookiness' and paradoxicality of action-at-a-distance.

This paper is an attempt to synthesise the two theories in what is felt to be that obviously logical and natural way.
