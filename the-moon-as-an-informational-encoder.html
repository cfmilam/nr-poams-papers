The Moon as an Informational Encoder

N. Vivian Pope

A discussion piece for the NRIG

Len Moran writes:

Dear Viv,

You stated in your paper:

"And if someone asks 'But in the end, where does all this information come from?' to this the answer is that it comes from nowhere outside the system. The system is self-contained, the only analysis of this information that is sensible to contemplate are the objects themselves. That is, in the last within the ultimate encoders and decoders of the information that circulates in the informational matrix."

I am getting confused over what you mean by objects as a source. Does the moon (as an object) exist as a source of encoded quantum information? If so, in what sense has it been encoded? To encode something means to start off with a given state, then encode it to allow transmission and then decode to produce the original state. Following this analogy, what exactly is the moon before it is encoded? I seem to be in a loop, in that the moon as a macrophenomenal object resulting from the encoded quantum information that in turn is derived from the macrophenomenal object. A bit like the chicken and egg scenario.

As usual, any clarification would be welcome.
Best regards,
Len

Viv replies:

Dear Len,

The form of a thing, or object, signals what it is and, of course, so does its behaviour in some way. A peacock displays its plumage and struts before a peahen. This you can regard as an 'encoding' by the male bird and the recognition of that form and behaviour, by the female, as 'decoding'. An object like the moon does not, of course, deliberately encode its form and behaviour in that way, though a signaller at a distance were to light a beacon or wave a semaphore flag, but the result is the same as that.

From our particular science-philosophical point of view, the object, in this case, is a macrophenomenon, or macroscopic pattern, composed of microphenomenal elements. In the video-screen analogy we often use, the object is like a recognisable picture composed of patterns and sequences of screen-pixel events. How does the picture 'encode' itself in those micro-events? It doesn't. Rather, it is the micro-events (quantum events) that encode the macro-object (the picture). And vice versa, the macro-picture constrains and orders the micro-events. This is the essence of holism — the parts and the whole mutually define each other.

So the moon, as a macrophenomenal object, is the integrated pattern of all the quantum-informational events by which it is constituted and perceived. The 'encoding' is not a temporal process of first having a moon and then encoding it. The moon is the encoded pattern. There is no moon 'before' encoding, any more than there is a picture on a video-screen 'before' the pixel-events. The two are co-constitutive. This is why the chicken-and-egg analogy, while tempting, does not quite apply. In the informational view, the chicken and the egg are aspects of one and the same informational process.

Best,
Viv
