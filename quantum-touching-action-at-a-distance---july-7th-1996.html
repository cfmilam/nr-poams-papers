A Simple Cinematic Deduction of Relativity The standard consequences of special relativity can be deduced from the cinematic model in the following very simple way. As has now been seen, the constant c need not be conceived as the speed of 'photons' in a primordial vacuum. It can, without contradiction, be regarded as the upper unreachable limit on the kinematical speeds of objects in observational. fiameworks. At every stage in their motion, these objects are instantaneously and reciprocally quantum-connected by those angular momentum balancing-influences already described. For instance, according to the cinematic model, alllnotions of objects are changes in their successive positions in those 'still' dimensions w'hich are, as it were, 'lateral'to that of their'longitudinal' time-succession. So both the change in posrtion and the time (proper time) taken for that change are, uniformly, time-measures in seconds.
