What POAMS Has Achieved (2nd Draft)

N. Vivian Pope & Anthony D. Osborne — 2004

Science has not got to where it is today through a steady unbroken line of logical deduction. Its history has been, mostly, a trail of circuitous, inductive meanderings based on chance discoveries made in different and mostly unrelated observational circumstances. The conceptions that have been forged to explain these discoveries, illustrious though they may be, have been almost entirely ad hoc, with little or no concern for overall logical or philosophical coherence.

However, it is found that in retrospect there is a distinct logical thread running through these discoveries of the sort that might be expected of nature as opposed to our conventional conceptions of it. Although this could not have been seen at the time, it is clear enough when examined in hindsight.

POAMS presents for public scrutiny the results of this retrospective enquiry. To the studious and open-minded reader it reveals that many of the circumstantial explanations of phenomena produced in their day are not only logically redundant but are now also a barrier to any possibility of realising their natural conceptual coherence. This has produced a plethora of 'theories' and 'theories about theories', many of which – even some of the most famous of them – now serve as no more than conventional clutter in the path of scientific progress.

POAMS, then, is not to be seen as some 'new theory of physics'. To see it that way is completely to misunderstand it. In fact, it is more of an anti-theory, insofar as it makes maximum use of 'Ockham's razor' in shaving-off all the current and traditional rationalisations that prove, on logical analysis, to be surplus to requirements.

Undoubtedly, some scholars will see this as a scurrilous 'attack' on our scientific traditions. They will be offended by what they may regard as a lack of proper respect and veneration for our intellectual heritage. Indeed, some of these 'concept conservationists' may regard it as tantamount to blasphemy. (One professor has even declared it 'immoral'.) Sooner or later, however, what can be done will be done. ('Truth will out,' as it is said.)

The following is the distinct logical thread that POAMS has uncovered, running straight through all the loose conventional 'fluff'. The standpoint from which this analysis is undertaken is that of Normal Realism. This is a form of radical empiricism, or phenomenalism, according to which physical phenomena, if properly interpreted with an absolute minimum of theoretical and metaphysical overlay, explain themselves. In other words, Normal Realism is a logical holism. It holds that, whether we perceive it or not, everything in nature must be logically connected and coherent.

It is obvious that nature cannot misinterpret itself, so anything we see as puzzling or paradoxical has to be an illusion, a matter of either misinterpretation of sensory/instrumental information or misuse of language, or both. In any event, not even for the most meticulous of observers, do things in nature reveal themselves readily named and replete with instructions as to how they should be identified and classified. Things such as 'electrons', 'photons', 'quarks', 'quasars' and so on are more items of human language than of nature. If they behave as, logically, our language leads us to expect, then for that moment we are lucky. However, no logical or mathematical form of language that has yet been devised has maintained its logical integrity for long. History reveals the extent to which even the very best, most promising projections of scientific genius have been prone to the necessity of review, and perhaps even radical revision, in the light of ongoing experience.

Normal Realism and conventional science, therefore, work in literally opposite directions: science from the past towards the future, and Normal Realism – as a preparation for the future – from the present to the past. ('Step back to leap the further,' as it is said.) This means returning to historical 'square one' so as to contemplate from then on an alternative, ahistorical track forwards.

Socrates used to say that he had no ideas, opinion or theories of his own, but that by a process of dialectical analysis he would examine the beliefs of others and point out, for the benefit of their proponents, any logical gaps or contradictions those beliefs might contain. As he might have said (in the modern vernacular), 'I don't know what is right, true or real, but I sure as hell know what isn't.' And that, in a nutshell, is the defining mantra of the Normal Realist.

In short, Normal Realism resurrects what used to be called 'The Search for Truth', and any modern sophist who declares that there is no such thing as truth is asked to explain how that statement can be true without contradiction.

What, then, is this truth that, by its Socratic process of theoretical elimination, Normal Realism and its minimalist mathematical spin-off have uncovered? The fine details of this uncovering are available elsewhere (see websites www.poams.org). For the moment, the following is a schematic picture of the 'necklace', as it were, of the POAMS achievements so far, together with the strings of pure logic on which they are strung.

First is the discovery, concurred and approved by Herman Bondi in 1965, that the conventional 'velocity of light', $c$, is not a velocity at all but is simply a scale constant for interconverting conventional units of metres and seconds in the measurement of space-distances $s$.

Second is the application of that constant, $c$, to all three dimensions of space, expressing them as measures $s/c$, in seconds to include time, also in seconds, as the natural fourth dimension of ordinary observational experience.

Third is the extension of Pythagoras' theorem from the usual two and three spatial dimensions to include time, all within a four-dimensional time-triangle, the hypotenuse of which gives a measure of the time-dilation of a moving body relative to the observer that turns out to be identical to that given by Einstein's Special Theory of Relativity. In this way, POAMS goes straight from Pythagoras to Einstein. This bypasses all the theoretical superstructure of the conventional 'speed of light' theory and all the puzzles this generated at the time of Faraday and Maxwell, and others such as Lorentz, and Michelson & Morley. Indeed it bypasses the whole tradition of 'electrodynamics' as conceived during that era.

Next, since frequency is the inverse of time, it was anticipated that there might be a direct logical, non-theoretical connection between the relativistic time-formula and the formula for spectral frequencies discovered by Balmer and Rydberg. And so it transpired. The time-formula, expressed in Diophantine terms of whole numbers, or quanta, with the dimensions of energy, or action, do provide, by pure syllogistic, precisely the formula for discrete spectral frequencies that Balmer and Rydberg discovered by numerical trial and error.

By that same method of anti-theoretical syllogistic, another direct logical connection was uncovered. This was between the frequency formula of Balmer and Rydberg and the energy levels of the schematic Bohr atom. A further purely logical route was then revealed between the time-formula and, not only the exact values of the mass constituents of the Bohr atom (the so-called 'electron' and 'proton') but also the standard orbital velocity and radius parameters of those masses. All this was, again, without any involvement with traditional 'electrodynamics'. The latter was achieved by the logical elimination of all purely theoretical and invisible in vacuo 'forces'. This was by the simple process of cashing-out the conventional 'static charge' of the 'electron', in coulombs, in kinetic energy units of joules, to match the spin kinetic energy ascribed to the 'electron' by Goudsmit and Uhlenbeck.

This follows from another uncovering, by POAMS, of the fact that Newton's first law of motion is completely unempirical. To declare, with one 'law' that all force-free motion is rectilinear and then, via another 'law' (i.e., that of 'universal gravitation') to explain why nowhere do bodies obey that first law, creates a whole plethora of invisible and undetectable 'forces' held accountable for natural, non-rectilinear (e.g., orbital) motion. More directly empirical, therefore, than Newton's straight-line ('inertial') momentum $mv$ is angular momentum $mvr$, which POAMS demonstrates to be sufficient in itself to account for observed orbital motion without having to postulate the usual opposition between two opposing 'centrifugal' and 'centripetal' (i.e., 'straight-line' and 'gravitational') invisible forces.

The fact that angular momentum is an overall-conserved quantity is then sufficient to explain why masses move in relation to one another in the correlated, paired and balanced arrangements they do, for the simple reason that to move in any other way would be to disconserve their overall angular momentum. By the same token, the orbital parameters of spinning masses cannot be the same as those of non-spinning ones, so that masses (such as that of the so-called 'electron'), the proportion of whose spin angular momentum to its orbital angular momentum is large, have to describe much tighter and more energetic orbits. Indeed, calculations prove that for a pair of pure masses, of the values calculated for the 'electron' and 'proton', respectively, the orbital parameters are precisely those ascribed by Bohr on classical 'electrodynamical' principles.

In effect, then, POAMS circumvents, by pure syllogistic, and without any theoretical elaboration, all the customary theoretical paraphernalia of 'electrodynamics' and the ideas of 'field-forces' that were spawned by Newton's illustrious but logically unnecessary creation of his two opposing 'rectilinear' and 'gravitational' laws. Thus, POAMS dispenses, at a stroke, with the chronic problem of 'Unified Field' by getting rid of all 'fields' and 'field-forces' altogether.

It also uncovers another direct logical route from time-dilation in rectilinear 'inertial space' to time-dilation in natural force-free orbital motion. Expressing the standard time-dilation formula in terms of its $v^2$ and substituting the resulting expression for that $v^2$ in the angular momentum equation directly produces a formula for orbital time-dilation of the kind that is observed in GPS and other satellites. When fully articulated (by Osborne), the formula predicts, to an extent approaching a hundred percent accuracy, the adjustments that the GPS and GLONASS engineers have to make in their satellite clocks. The formula also predicts the slowing of clocks and other time-processes as they approach large masses. This is in the same way that is predicted by Einstein's General Relativity, but without involving any of that theory's conventional rationale.

Moreover, since angular momentum analyses-out to ultimately irreducible units of $h/2\pi$, it is automatically quantised. In opposition, therefore, to the 'gravitationally warped space-time continuum' of General Relativity, POAMS presents an angular momentum discretum with equivalent metrical features – of an angular momentum nexus, not 'gravitation' – which disposes of the perennial problem of how to reconcile Relativistic continuity with Quantum discontinuity.

In addition to this, the POAMS formulation reveals that the orbits of bodies are never closed but that they perform a 'rosette' pattern of precession, of the sort first observed with the planet Mercury. It also produces a formula, equivalent in angular momentum terms to the Schwarzschild radius, as the limit to which masses may approach one another without the consequence of merging in what is conventionally called 'gravitational collapse'.

However, in the POAMS formula there are no 'singularities' of that metaphysical sort, whether they be those of the alleged 'black holes' or the 'Big Bang'. 'Black holes' are explained as the empty and therefore non-light-emitting barycentres, or centres-of-moment between orbiting masses, and the 'Big Bang' is rejected in favour of a long-neglected logical alternative to the conventional 'galactic recession' interpretation of the Hubble redshift.

The fact that all recessional motions are accompanied by redshifts does not entail that all redshifts signify motions of recession, hence the almost universal assumption (among cosmologists) that they do is, by definition, a dogma. This leaves, wide open, the logical possibility, which POAMS projects, that the redshift is a manifestation of relativistic time-dilations in distant galaxies due to their increasing random velocities as they approach with respect to the greater and greater accumulated mass of the cosmos with increasing distance relative to the observer. Calculations made on the basis of the average mass-density given by astronomers for astronomical space support this.

Aside from these theoretical departures at the speculative edges of our astronomical observations, POAMS does all that both Special and General Relativity do, but in a single, logically unified and philosophically coordinated way. With its built-in quantisation, the synthesis also automatically includes the basis of the Quantum Theory of Planck and Bohr. This removes the long-standing 'EPR' conflict between quantum instantaneity and relativistic time-delay by presenting light as a logical combination of those two instantaneous and time-delayed components. This is by analogy with a movie, in which, without any suggestion of contradiction, the action consists of a time-delayed series of instantaneously occurring photographic frames or 'stills'.

In all of this, the need for 'theory' is pared to an absolute minimum – to zero, in some cases. This does not mean that the theories that are taught to students of current Physics and Mechanics should now be consigned, en bloc, to history – as though that were even remotely possible, anyway, given the societal upheaval it would require. For the sake of sheer pragmatic concern, therefore, the purely circumstantial ways in which the fine details of phenomena have been dealt with in the past, no matter how conceptually convoluted they may be, cannot be simply dispensed with 'just like that', as the comedian Tommy Cooper would say. For such reasons, the current textbook explanations would need to be retained until such time as the theoretically expurgated logical synthesis might be fully articulated to reach into all these specialised nooks and crannies.

However, the knowledge that is available, for the taking, that there exists a clear logical bypass around the theoretical congestion that has now all but brought common understanding to a halt, might reverse the current trend in Education whereby Physics, once the most prestigious of university subjects, has now been relegated to something like the least popular in the curriculum.

Most controversial – indeed, as some will undoubtedly see it, the most incredible – consequence of all, however, is that POAMS completely inverts the customary way of thinking of light-in-space to thinking of the space being in the light. When we come to think of it, it is plain that light, in its fullest spectral range, from deepest infra-red to farthest ultraviolet and gamma, without remainder, is the ultimate source of all our knowledge of nature, from ordinary commonsense to science and cosmology. No less plain is that all the rest is interpretation or else just pure theory.

Einstein's Second Postulate which separates light as we see it from the seen objects (called 'Einstein separation') is one of those postulates that POAMS proves to be logically redundant. This is on the grounds that, as stated above, in its own intrinsic, or 'proper' time, each quantum of the light is instantaneous, hence is as much a constituent of the object as of our seeing it. The 'space' we traditionally interpose between ourselves, as decoders of light-information, and the objects that are the 'sources' of that information are therefore projections out of the information itself.

Thus, all the three spatial dimensions, plus the inter-dependent time dimension, with all our knowledge of the masses, movements and other characteristics of physical objects framed in those dimensions – including even the time-delay of light-information over distance, as traditionally conceived – are all observational extrapolations out of the informational patterns and sequences of quantum light-pixels, with all those perspectival characteristics that are known first (i.e., logically a priori) to the science of optics (minus only 'light-velocity') and then, secondarily (a posteriori), to the sciences of physics and mechanics. This restores 'relativity' to the meaning originally defined by Mach.

In this phenomenological way, the quanta, to which all light-energy ultimately reduce, cease to be conceived as space-travelling particles ('photons') and become, instead, the ultimate informational 'pixels' out of which, as informational decoder, the observer projects the world around him. With other observers doing the same, with all their ancillary instruments of observation, the objectivity of nature is then revealed as the projection, couched in human language, of a whole observational community. This replaces the presumptuous and ubiquitous 'God's-eye-view' of classical science.

This resulting approach to science and epistemology is therefore what might be called 'objective empiricism' – which, virtually, is what Normal Realism is all about. The complete inversion, or revolution, that is thus implied is to base physical science on actual digital or quantum information rather than on classical analog intuitions about objects as 'God' might be presumed to see them. This shift from 'analog' to 'digital' in the POAMS approach to physics brings that subject into line with the Informational Revolution that is already gaining pace throughout all other areas of technology. All that has prevented this merger, so far, as POAMS analyses it, is the 'Einstein separation' that was theoretically interposed between the world and our seeing of it, which POAMS proves to be not only unnecessary but also a long-standing barrier to any commonsense understanding of true relativity and its natural relation to the quantum.

Something else worth mentioning is what was spotted by several readers of the POAMS literature. This was that the switch from a mechanics-based to an information-based science removes the traditional barrier between material science and the humanities. No longer can consciousness be regarded as the 'ghost in the machine', an incidental, no-account spin-off from fundamental mechanical processes. Instead, consciousness and communication become the very essence of reality in all its forms, especially in observation-based, or relativistic, physics. This, as those readers perceived it, removes the traditional barrier between material science and the humanities, connecting the two seamlessly together.

Moreover, it was pointed out that if the universe were a Machine, then since there can be no morality in a machine and if we and our society are no more than parts of that Machine, there can be no morality in our society. Any appearance of morality and responsibility therefore has to be an illusion, since anything we do or decide is determined by our atoms. Besides, so far as religion is concerned, it makes no sense to worship a Machine, far less make supplication to it in prayer.

So POAMS, as these people perceive it, cures the underlying schizophrenia of our Western society, manifest as it now is on all hands, between its twin bases: materialist science on the one hand and its Christian values on the other. This, they judge, should restore to our society some measure, at least, of the commonsense morality that a total preoccupation with the purely material benefits of classical science and technology has now all but excluded.

Has POAMS, then, succeeded in uncovering the truth as Socrates envisioned it, behind all the accumulated and ingrained speculative beliefs of contemporary science? Well, if this purely logical, minimalist interpretation of the actual phenomena is not the truth that Socrates sought, then the onus falls on anyone who may not think so to point out, by diligent study of the POAMS literature (see website), some definite flaw in the logic and tell us what is.
