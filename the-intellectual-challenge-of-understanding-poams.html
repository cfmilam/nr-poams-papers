You thought that understanding Einstein's Theory of Relativity was a mental challenge. Well, try this.

Central to modern physics is a notorious and (as many see it, altogether scandalous) clash between two entirely different and seemingly antagonistic theoretical approaches to physics. These are Relativity and Quantum Theory. The one approach is that of the followers of Albert Einstein and the other is that of the followers of Niels Bohr. This continuing, unresolved clash is befween relativistic time-delay and quantum instantaneity and is known as the 'EPR paradox' [2]. In its simplest terms. this is the apparent contradiction between, on the one hand, Einstein's claim. on the basis of his famous Theory of Relativity, that no physical influence can travel faster than the speed of light and, on the other hand, the claim of the quantum theorists, after Bohr, that there are quantum interactions that have to be instantaneous regardless of distance. The solution to this problem, which POAMS proffers, is altogether simple and logical but, as it transpires. extremely difficult to get one's mind around. (Good logic, it has been said. may be bad psychology.) This mental difficulty has been named 'the intellectual problem'. It is the same problem that has attended just about every revolutionary move that was ever made in the history of science, which is that of having to make progress against the psychological back-pressure generated by prior presumptions in favour of the more generally accepted and familiar ways of thinking. This force-of-habit backlash makes it difficult, in the process of coping with radically new ideas. of keeping one's train of thoughts on a strict logical track without being derailed by habitual assumptions and precepts.

What POAMS does is to demonstrate that in fact there is simply no problem, that a light-interaction between a distant atom in what we call the 'source' and another atom in our eye or observing instrument which we call the 'sink' is both instantaneous, as quantum theory directs, gttg! time-delayed as Relativity requires. This raises the problem of how, mentally, to accommodate this apparently contradictory state of affairs. Not everyone, it seems, can grasp the solution. For some it remains permanently insoluble. For others, it takes a while for the conceptual 'Eureka!'to occur. They report that it is like suddenly seeing the point of a joke when the penny drops, as it were.

Now. as if that solution were not mentally testing enough, then what about this? Let's say that itis the Copernican 'full Monty', the complete mental revolution. It has been suggested. by the way, that this should be accompanied by a 'health waming' for those of a nervous disposition. Let that warning now be taken as read. In classical physics it was assumed that somehow, there is a 'universe' of self-sufficient space, like a kind of dimensional 'grid' consisting of three selfsufficient dimensions of extension, all enduring in a single overall 'universal' time, a time which is the same everywhere throughout. Into this dimensional receptacle there are somehow sprinkled bits of matter which move relatively to one another in those sorts of ways which Newton's and other's mechanistic theories describe. These mechanisms, it was assumed, are completely self-sufficient, having little or nothing to do with our perceptions of them. This generates what is called the epistemological fallacy. which is the age-old conundrum as to how we can be certain of the existence of these objects - or even sensibly talk about them - other than on the basis of knowledge obtained in our perceiving them.

So far as POAMS is concerned, there never was any logical or empirical justification for that assumption of the existence of these self-sufficient entities, far less as a basis for empirical science. All it ever was, in the final analysis, was pure theoretical fancy. There simply is no way - as if it needed saying - in which we can know of the existence of anything, its distance from ourselves and from other things. its mass. shape. physical composition and other qualities and characteristics, other than on the basis of some kind of direct or indirect, instrumental observation, either by ourselves or in communication with others in the observational community. Here, then, is the 'Copemican full Monty'. As conventional modem physics describes it. on the ultimate microphysical level, atoms interact in little quantum bursts of energy called 'photons'. How they do this at a distance when, according to Einstein. the proper times of those photons are instantaneous (as they are for anything travelling at the 'speed of light') is a complete mystery. The POAMS paraphrase of that conventional statement is that on the ultimate microphenomenal level of observation there is no space and no ttme but only sporadic occurrences of little bursts quanta. So far as POAMS is concerned, it is from of quantum energy "ujl.d informational pattems and sequences of these quantum events, occurring in observation like the pixel-events on a video-screen, that space, time and all the other defining dimensions and properties of the physical scenario exist.
