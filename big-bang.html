Big Bang

N. Vivian Pope

A Logical Analysis of The ‘Big Bang’ Theory The ‘Big Bang’ theory of cosmic creation is surely the silliest theory on record. There is nothing in history to touch it, not even the idea that the World sits on the backs of elephants standing on an island in an infinitely extended cosmic sea.

That does, at least present a picture, which is coherent in its way. As we shall see, the ‘Big Bang’ theory can’t even claim that. What is most amazing is to contemplate that this theory has ‘caught on’ in science, both popular and professional, to the extent that science textbooks present the theory as though it were incontrovertible fact.

But what is the basis of this theory? It is the true fact, unearthed by two astronomers, Edwin Hubble and Vesto Slipher, in the early 1900s, that the distant galaxies exhibit what are called red shifts in their light-spectra in proportion to their distance.

This has suggested to some scientists that those galaxies are receding from us at speeds which are increasing with that distance, thus signifying an ‘expansion’ of the whole universe, and this idea has been imbibed by the public, big time! But on what is that assumption founded?

It is certainly not an observed fact that those galaxies are receding. They are much too far away from us to observe anything like that. Nor is it possible to gauge that motion in any of the normal ways for moving objects. For instance there is no diminution in size, over time, that can possibly be observed and, unquestionably, there is no other form of perspective or parallax.

Nor are there any of the other natural accompaniments of the phenomenon which should suggest motion. So, what makes us think that those galaxies are receding is nothing more nor less than the fact of the reddening in the appearance of the galaxies in proportion to their estimated distance.

This has been interpreted as a ‘Doppler effect’, like the sound of a passing, fast-moving vehicle shifting in pitch from higher to lower, as it approaches and speeds away. And because the galaxies display that drop in pitch in their light spectra, as compared to what those spectra should be if their sources were relatively close by, it is inferred that this reddening is essentially a ‘Doppler shift’, hence irrefutable evidence of recessional motion.

Now how many sensible people would argue that because all criminals are human beings, all human beings are criminals? Ridiculous, you would say. So why is it not regarded as ridiculous to argue that because all receding objects display a reddening of their light, any observed reddening in the appearance of the galaxies signifies the ‘fact’ that it is receding?

Anyway, what a huge assumption that is, based, not only on such scant evidence but also on an interpretation that is so obviously fallacious! But, it will be said, since there are no viable alternatives to the ‘Doppler’ interpretation of the phenomenon, how else can that galactic reddening be explained?

Like it or despise it, they say, the interpretation of the reddening of their light as a galactic recession is a Hobson’s choice. Logically, however, that claim can be made only when all other possible explanations are exhausted. Anything less makes the claim a dogma, by definition.

Moreover, there really are alternative logical explanations of the reddening phenomenon that have not been refuted. One, for instance, interprets the reddening as a relativistic time-dilation due to motions that are omnidirectional, or random, increasing in speed with increasing distance in a universal nexus of angular

momentum, as the theory explains. This theory predicts precisely the sort of general reddening that is observed in the case of the galaxies without any suggestion of an overall Doppler effect, far less any hint of an ‘expanding universe.[1] Unless this and other such theories are fully addressed and refuted, the ‘expanding universe’ can in no way be claimed as an ‘observed fact’.

Anyway, on the supposition that cosmical expansion is a fact, from the alleged ‘speed’ of the expansion the implication is unavoidable that, winding backwards some thirteen or so billion years, we come to – what? What else but the lunatic suggestion that the whole universe, with all its prodigious energy was once packed into single point, so that, unable to contain all that energy, the universe must have burst out, all those billions of years ago, in a prodigious and unimaginable ‘Big Bang’.

Now how can that possibly be? Surely, in Science’s more rational days, that logical consequence would have been seen as the reductio ad absurdum of the whole ‘expansion’ interpretation, to be rejected forthwith! But, no, it is much more exciting to ignore that logical rule and embrace that absurdity as a scientific discovery.

In this way, what is plainly scientific fiction becomes hailed as scientific ‘fact’. But now, in the name of sanity, forget all the scientific and popular hype, Let’s just sit and think, calmly and objectively, with our commonsense hats on, whether or not that theory is really credible.

The idea of the whole universe having started out as a ‘Big Bang’ at a single cosmical point of creation, is just logically and linguistically incoherent. For instance, where is that point supposed to have been situated, and in relation to what? And if that whole universe is expanding, then into what is it expanding and at what rate, since all space and time have to be, by definition, inside that theoretical point?

And by that same token, the answer to what the expansion is ‘into’ has to be ‘into nowhere’, since outside that point has to be a dimensionless, property-less and quality-less, unspeakable nothing, that is not even empty space. But how, literally, can something ‘expanding into nowhere’ be said to be ‘expanding’?

Imagine the following bit of conversation: ‘Into what is the Universe expanding?’ ‘Nowhere.’ ‘I see, so it is not expanding.’ ‘Oh no, it is expanding. ‘But into nowhere?’ ‘Yes. ‘Eh!’ Besides, with all the measuring apparatus being potentially, inside that assumed punctiform Universe, with reference to what can that ‘Universe’ possibly be said to be either small or large?

Any talk of ‘size’ in this context is just pure nonsense. So, what sense can be made of the statement that the size of the ‘primeval atom was somewhere between the size of a pinhead and the size of an apple’? Also, since there are no outside clocks or any other sorts of chronometers, mechanical, atomic or whatever, to register any passage of time, at what rate can that ‘point’ be regarded as ‘expanding’?

The whole idea is flagrant misuse of language, like Lewis Carroll’s deliberately nonsensical example, ‘Why is a mouse when it spins?’ Even as a figment of the imagination, the idea of the ‘expanding universe’, when we really think about

it, is meaningless. The whole idea is simply a complete and utter shambles. What more can be said on that score? But, of course, some scientists will claim that, despite how nonsensical it may appear, there are all sorts of bits of evidence that support the ‘Big Bang theory.

For instance, there is the three degrees Kelvin microwave background radiation discovered by Penzias and Wilson in 1965, thereafter claimed to be a primordial relic of the ‘Big Bang’. Then there is the abundance of helium in the universe which, it is claimed, cannot be made by stars, hence is another relic of the original ‘Big Bang’.

Another, extremely flimsy, bit of ‘evidence’ is claimed to be Olbers’ paradox, according to which, if the universe were not expanding, all the enclosed to-ing and fro-ing of light would make the sky infinitely bright, not dark as we know it. This ignores the fact that in quantum theory there is no to-ing and fro-ing of light in any way that could produce such a consequence. [2] And, last but not least, is the little girl’s eminently sensible but scientifically unanswerable question: ‘Where did the Big Bang come from?’ (Shades of the ‘Emperor’s new cloak’ fable here!)

However, there is, in Psychology, the well known phenomenon of ‘dissonance reduction’, a prime example of which is the way defence lawyers, to convince themselves of the innocence of their client, have to grasp at all sorts of peripheral ‘facts’ that ‘prove’ the innocence of that person who, in their heart of hearts they may know to be guilty.

Even in the best circles of science, there are numerous examples of that kind of self-deception, which is nothing new [3]. Yet it is astounding how many people can believe implicitly in the ‘Big Bang’ theory of cosmical creation based on ideas which are logically untenable.

But there are psychological reasons for this, also, such as, mainly, the need to conform. So, to be a socially well-balanced individual, one has to ‘go with the flow’. So, if ‘lunacy’ is the ‘going rate, then best become ‘lunatic’ oneself for social acceptability.

And who can confidently declare, in view of some of the present ‘scientific’ theories so flagrantly displayed in practically all sectors of the media, such as the ‘Big Bang’, ‘black holes’, ‘wormholes’ between parallel universes, and God knows what else, that this ‘Gadarene descent’ of modern science into complete nonsense is not already well under way?
